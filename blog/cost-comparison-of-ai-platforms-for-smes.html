Cost Comparison of AI Platforms for SMEs

25 November 2026

This cost comparison of AI platforms for SMEs sets out what ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot charge a team, using the prices each provider publishes itself. We checked every figure on the providers’ own pricing pages on 18 November 2026. Prices change often, so confirm them before you buy.

Quick answer: For a team, ChatGPT Business and the Claude Team plan both cost $20 per user per month billed annually, or $25 billed monthly. Google Workspace with Gemini runs from £5.90 to £18.40 per user per month, and Microsoft 365 Business plans with Copilot cost £18.10 or £24.60 per user per month paid yearly. Tax is extra on all of them. Prices were checked on 18 November 2026.

Key Takeaways

  • ChatGPT Business and Claude Team are priced the same: $20 per user per month billed annually, or $25 billed monthly.

  • Gemini and Copilot come with office software: you pay for Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, and the AI is included or added on.

  • None of the published prices include VAT or other tax.

  • Annual billing is cheaper on every platform, but it commits you for a year.

  • Usage beyond the included limits can cost extra, so check how each plan handles heavy use.

What Each Platform Charges

ChatGPT

OpenAI’s Help Centre lists ChatGPT Plus, the plan for one person, at $20 a month. ChatGPT Business is the plan for teams. A Standard seat costs $25 per user per month on a monthly plan, or $20 per user per month billed annually, and a workspace needs at least two seats.

Premium seats, with higher usage limits, cost $125 monthly or $100 billed annually. OpenAI says pricing may vary depending on your country and currency, and that it will not train on a Business workspace’s data.

Claude

Anthropic’s pricing page shows a free plan, and Pro for individuals at $20 a month, or $17 a month with annual billing ($200 up front). The Team plan is for 2 to 150 people. A Standard seat costs $25 per seat per month, or $20 billed annually. Premium seats, with five times the usage, cost $125 monthly or $100 billed annually.

The Team plan includes central billing and admin controls, and no model training on your content by default. Enterprise pricing starts at 20 seats and adds usage charged at API rates. Anthropic notes that the prices shown do not include applicable tax.

Gemini (Google Workspace)

Google sells Gemini for business as part of Google Workspace. On the UK pricing page, Business Starter is £5.90, Business Standard £11.80 and Business Plus £18.40 per user per month with a one-year commitment, before tax. Google was also advertising 50% off for the first three months when we checked.

What you get differs by plan. Starter includes the Gemini assistant in Gmail and chat in the Gemini app. Standard adds Gemini in Docs, Meet and other Workspace apps. Google states that your company data is not used to train models. These plans can be bought for a maximum of 300 users.

Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft sells Copilot for business in two ways. You can buy a Microsoft 365 plan with Copilot built in: Business Standard with Copilot is £18.10 per user per month paid yearly, and Business Premium with Copilot is £24.60. Paying monthly costs £21.72 and £29.52.

If you already have an eligible Microsoft 365 Business plan, the Copilot Business add-on is £13.80 per user per month paid yearly. That is an offer price for the first year on purchases made by 31 December 2026, and the usual price is £16.10. Paid monthly, it is £19.32. All of these prices exclude VAT.

Microsoft also says Copilot Chat is included at no additional cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans, and that some advanced AI features are billed by usage.

A Worked Example: Five People

Here is what a team of five would pay each month on annual billing, using the published prices above and before tax:

  • ChatGPT Business: 5 x $20 = $100 a month

  • Claude Team: 5 x $20 = $100 a month

  • Google Workspace Business Standard: 5 x £11.80 = £59 a month

  • Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot: 5 x £18.10 = £90.50 a month

These are not like-for-like. The Google and Microsoft figures include email, storage and office apps, which you may be paying for already. ChatGPT and Claude are standalone AI assistants that sit alongside whatever office software you use. The dollar prices will also move with the exchange rate on your card.

Costs Beyond the Monthly Fee

Tax and Currency

Every price in this guide is shown before tax. For platforms priced in dollars, the amount in pounds depends on the exchange rate when you are charged, and OpenAI says pricing can vary by country.

Annual Commitments and Offer Prices

The lowest prices are tied to annual billing or a one-year commitment. Offer prices, such as Google’s three-month discount and Microsoft’s first-year add-on price, end. Work out the full-price cost for year two before you commit.

Usage Limits and Extra Credits

Team plans include a set amount of usage. OpenAI lets Business workspaces buy credits for usage beyond the included limits. Anthropic sells Premium seats with more usage. Microsoft bills some advanced features by usage. If a few people on your team will use AI heavily, ask how that is charged.

Time to Set Up and Learn

The subscription is only part of the cost. Someone has to set up accounts, agree how the tool will be used and show the team how to get good results. Our guide to prompt engineering best practices is a good place to start.

Data Protection: What to Check Before You Sign Up

If your team will put personal data into an AI tool, such as customer names or emails, UK data protection law applies to how you use it. The Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) is the UK regulator and publishes advice for small organisations on its website.

Before you choose a plan, check three things on the provider’s own pages: whether your content is used to train its models, who in your team can see what, and what admin controls you get. On the pages we checked, OpenAI and Google say business plan data is not used to train their models, and Anthropic says the same applies by default on its Team plan. Microsoft lists enterprise data protection among the features of its Copilot plans.

How to Choose Between Them

  • Start with the software you already use. If your team works in Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, the AI built into those tools needs the least change.

  • List the jobs you want help with. Drafting, research, summarising meetings and analysing spreadsheets suit different tools.

  • Try before you commit. Use a free plan or a monthly plan first, then move to annual billing once you know it is being used.

  • Count the real number of users. A plan with a two-seat minimum is fine for most teams, but a sole trader may only need an individual plan.

  • Check the price again on the day. Every figure here was correct on 18 November 2026 and may have changed since.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI platform is cheapest for a UK SME?

On the prices published on 18 November 2026, Google Workspace Business Starter has the lowest price per user at £5.90 a month, but Gemini only works in Gmail and the Gemini app on that plan. For a standalone AI assistant, ChatGPT Business and the Claude Team plan both start at $20 per user per month when billed annually. The cheapest plan is not always the best fit, so compare what each one includes.

Do these prices include VAT?

No. Microsoft says its UK prices do not include VAT, Google says its prices do not include tax, and Anthropic says the prices shown for Claude do not include applicable tax. Check the final figure at checkout.

Is there a minimum number of users?

For the team plans, yes. A ChatGPT Business workspace needs at least two seats, and the Claude Team plan is for 2 to 150 people. Google Workspace Business plans can be bought for up to 300 users.

Are there free AI platforms an SME can start with?

Yes. ChatGPT and Claude both have free plans with limited usage, and Microsoft says Copilot Chat is included at no additional cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans. Free plans suit trying a tool out. The paid team plans add shared workspaces, admin controls and central billing.

How often should an SME review its AI platform costs?

Review them before each annual renewal and whenever a provider changes its plans. Several prices in this guide are promotional or tied to annual billing, so the figure you pay in year two can be different from year one.

A cost comparison of AI platforms for SMEs is only as good as its prices, so treat this guide as a starting point and check each provider’s pricing page before you buy. If you spot a figure that has changed, or have a question about this guide, contact our editorial team.
